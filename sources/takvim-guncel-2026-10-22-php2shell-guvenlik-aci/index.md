_Curator's reading of the capture._

Takvim (Turkey, Turkish), 22 October, updated 23:35: a five-question explainer on the crisis that closed public sites in Europe and what it means in Turkey. It says that after a warning from Belgium's Cyber Security Centre (CCB) the websites of the Foreign, Finance and Labour ministries and media and education platforms were temporarily disabled; that in the Netherlands DigiD, the Port of Rotterdam portal, the Schiphol airport app and House of Representatives sites were closed; that prefectures, police units and many public portals in France, Germany and Finland were closed as a precaution; that South Korea raised its cyber crisis alert and the US (FBI, NSA) and Japan made emergency update calls. In Turkey, the Presidency's Cyber Security Directorate issued national security alert TR-26-1372 to public and private infrastructure and said that technical checks of e-Devlet, which holds data on over 69 million citizens, found no risk or disruption. The paper's tone is reassuring.
