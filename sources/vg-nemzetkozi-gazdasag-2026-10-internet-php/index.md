_Curator's reading of the capture._

It says two serious flaws were enough for government portals, company services and Wikipedia's back-end to stop worldwide, with millions of attack attempts within a short time, cites a survey that PHP is used on 69.3% of sites whose server-side language is known, says Wiz's researchers found the flaws in August with AI tools and built working attack methods with Calif and OpenAI taking part in coordinating the fix and disclosure, and that patches appeared on 21 October.
