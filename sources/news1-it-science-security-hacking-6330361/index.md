#### What the page says

_Curator's reading of the capture._

A Korean news-agency feature (News1, 25 October) on small businesses unable to tell whether their sites are exposed. It reports that the two PHP flaws were disclosed on 21 October, that the government issued a cyber crisis 'caution' alert on 22 October, and that CYFIRMA said a Korean-speaking group had been discussing target selection and reconnaissance and planned a first target list by 25 October. It reports that PHP 8.1 and older no longer get official fixes, so shops on them must change version, that the hosting companies Cafe24 and Gabia applied patches (Gabia also backported fixes or mitigations to unsupported versions), and quotes two professors calling for public support for small firms. A KISA official says no attack cases had been reported to it and monitoring continues.

> 한국인터넷진흥원(KISA) 관계자는 이번 PHP 보안 취약점과 관련해 접수된 공격 사례는 없다며 모니터링을 지속하고 있다고 밝혔다.
