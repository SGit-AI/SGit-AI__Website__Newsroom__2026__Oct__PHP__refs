#### What the page says

_Curator's reading of the capture._

VTV (Vietnam, Vietnamese), 22 October: a news-desk item advising Vietnamese organisations using PHP to review systems and apply the patch. It says the flaws were found by Wiz's AI system, that many sites worldwide, including government systems, disconnected, and, citing no source, that the Netherlands and Finland recorded hackers beginning to exploit the flaws to break in. It quotes Wiz and OpenAI on AI's role, says no figure for Vietnam's exposure exists, and quotes Vu Ngoc Son of the National Cyber Security Association that this affects only web servers, that ordinary users need not worry directly, and that many providers patched at once.

> Đơn cử như Hà Lan và Phần Lan ghi nhận tin tặc đã bắt đầu khai thác các lỗ hổng này để xâm nhập vào hệ thống.
