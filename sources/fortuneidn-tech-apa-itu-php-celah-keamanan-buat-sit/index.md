Fortune Indonesia (Indonesian), an explainer of what PHP is and why the flaw matters. It says the PHP Foundation disclosed two flaws in PHP's core relating to memory management and error handling, that the US NSA said the flaws could let attackers reach files and databases without credentials, that the PHP Foundation said the fix was a challenge because it touches the core, risking application compatibility, performance and third-party extensions, and that the NSA urged all PHP-using organisations to update at once or disable vulnerable systems, with similar warnings from Japan, Singapore, Australia and the EU.
