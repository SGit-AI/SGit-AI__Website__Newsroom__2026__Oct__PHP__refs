#### What the page says

_Curator's reading of the capture._

Agenda Digitale (Italian), 22 October, by Giovanni Masi: an analysis of PHP2Shell and what the AI role means for companies and shared software. It gives W3Techs shares (PHP on 69.3% of sites whose server-side language is known; 64.5% of the first million, 61.0% of the first 100,000, 59.7% of the first 10,000, 59.3% of the first 1,000), says CERT-EU urged immediate updates, and relays the PHP Foundation's reconstruction: Calif's audit began on 30 July; on 11 August Ryan A reported an attack able to execute code remotely, the same day an independent analysis arrived from OpenAI, with further contributions from Wiz on 24 August and OpenAI on 18 September. It adds that Amazon Threat Intelligence puts an attempt on 17 October, MadPot sensors recorded new attempts on 21 October and Cloudflare saw hostile traffic and request changes to bypass countermeasures. It also relays that a leak on 20 October led PHP to bring the advisory and patches forward from 22 to 21 October, and that a video demonstration published by mistake and early circulation of already-patched software images made secrecy harder.
