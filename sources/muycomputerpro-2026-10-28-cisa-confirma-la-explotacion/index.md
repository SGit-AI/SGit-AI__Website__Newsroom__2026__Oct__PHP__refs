#### What the page says

_Curator's reading of the capture._

A Spanish IT-trade piece (MuyComputerPRO, 28 October) saying CISA has confirmed active exploitation of PHP2Shell and added both flaws to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog, ordering federal agencies to update before 29 October 2026. It places this after a Perplexity detection-and-response lead's confirmation on 24 October of remote code execution against honeypot systems, recounts that the issue began in a 2024 audit but was not shown to allow remote code execution until August, cites Censys for more than 32.51 million PHP services still on an affected version, and recommends PHP 8.2.35, 8.3.36, 8.4.27 or 8.5.12 and, until then, temporarily isolating exposed installations.
