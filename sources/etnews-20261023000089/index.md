Electronic Times (Korean), 23 October: the Korean firm Enki WhiteHat released a free diagnostic scanner and a vulnerability analysis report on PHP2Shell. The scanner checks the PHP version and related settings to give a first judgement of exposure, without using attack code; users enter a URL on a diagnostic site with no login. The report covers how the flaw arises, affected versions, mitigations and how to apply the official patch (8.5.12, 8.4.27, 8.3.36, 8.2.35); the firm plans to publish more on exposed-asset management.
