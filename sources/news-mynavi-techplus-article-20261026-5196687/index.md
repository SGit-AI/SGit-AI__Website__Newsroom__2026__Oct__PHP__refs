#### What the page says

_Curator's reading of the capture._

Mynavi News TECH+ (Japan, Japanese), 26 October: free support offers for PHP2Shell, from diagnosis to patching. It says Trend Micro published on 23 October a free risk-assessment tool, the 'PHP2Shell Exposure Checker', which takes a site's URL and judges possible exposure from externally visible information such as HTTP headers, sending no attack code and not testing exploitability, and also launched a seven-day free web-shielding service for SMEs through a cloud reverse proxy; TuxCare announced six months of free Linux and PHP protection for nonprofits registered in Japan (including patches for end-of-life PHP versions); HiroshimAI began free initial-response support for SMEs on 22 October (application deadline 31 October). It stresses the basic fix is a PHP update and that distributions may patch without changing the version string.
