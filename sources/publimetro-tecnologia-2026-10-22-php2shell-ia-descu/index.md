#### What the page says

_Curator's reading of the capture._

A Chilean news explainer (Publimetro Chile, published 21 October local time) that two critical flaws hidden for over a decade in PHP's code were detected with advanced AI from OpenAI and Wiz. It puts PHP at about 75% of sites whose technology is identifiable, relays the Wall Street Journal and Wiz on how the engine mishandles memory on fatal errors, says Calif used GPT-5.6 Sol to analyse PHP's source and Wiz detected the flaw independently, that OpenAI's Patch the Planet programme brought PHP developers, independent experts and infrastructure providers together for two months to patch before disclosure, that AI accelerated the proofs of concept from weeks to hours, and that the same capabilities could let malicious actors automate attack tools.

> La IA no solo fue determinante en la identificación del error, sino también en la aceleración de las pruebas de concepto.
