#### What the page says

_Curator's reading of the capture._

Revista Sociedade Militar (Brazil, Portuguese), 26 October: the site of the Brazilian Army's General Secretariat (SGEx), which publishes the Army's bulletins, showed a maintenance notice on Monday 26 October saying the page was in maintenance because of the PHP vulnerability. The magazine itself saw the notice on visiting the address. The rest is background from a BBC report: two flaws found with AI assistance by Calif and Wiz, emergency updates on 21 October, and precautionary suspensions by governments and companies abroad (Finland, the Netherlands, Edinburgh council, the BBC, the Wikimedia Foundation).
