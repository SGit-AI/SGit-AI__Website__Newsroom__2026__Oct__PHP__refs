#### What the page says

_Curator's reading of the capture._

A German local-news report (New-Facts.eu, 22 October) that the homepage of the Biberach district office has been offline since Wednesday 21 October because of the critical PHP security flaw. The authority says it took the site down as a precaution, that no systems of the district administration were hacked as far as is known and no danger exists, and that numerous municipalities, authorities, hospitals, universities and other public bodies have been affected since Wednesday afternoon. It names the two CVEs and the BSI's 9.2 rating and says the office is working with the IT companies involved to close the hole.
