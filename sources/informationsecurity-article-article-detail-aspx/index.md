_Curator's reading of the capture._

Information Security (資安人, Taiwan, Traditional Chinese), around 27 October: CISA added both PHP2Shell CVEs to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalogue on 26 October, confirming real-world exploitation, and the Brotli decoder flaw CVE-2026-111497 ('HTTP2Shell') keeps widening. It says Perplexity's Adel Karimi confirmed successful remote execution on his honeypot on 24 October, limited to a deliberately favourable single-worker mod_php setup; US federal agencies must update by 29 October; Censys estimates over 32.51 million PHP services still run affected versions; the PHP Foundation repackaged its official Windows builds for PHP 8.2 to 8.5 on 26 October (8.5.12-1, 8.4.27-1, 8.3.36-1, 8.2.35-1) to ship a patched Brotli 1.2.1, with the PHP2Shell fix unchanged; Microsoft issued .NET updates on 25 October; Imperva has shown remote execution for the Brotli flaw with a public proof of concept but no real-world exploitation is reported. The article is a compilation from The Hacker News and BleepingComputer.
