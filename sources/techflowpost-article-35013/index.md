#### What the page says

_Curator's reading of the capture._

A Chinese-language crypto-news essay (TechFlow, 30 October) titled 'From Binance to PHP2Shell', arguing that attackers increasingly target the whole chain of trust rather than cryptography. It opens with an alleged breach at a large exchange and other crypto thefts, which the page does not attribute to PHP2Shell; it then recaps PHP2Shell (two flaws scoring 9.2, PHP behind about 70% of sites, Palo Alto and Censys scans showing 22.6 million to 36.7 million exposed instances), says the flaws were found with AI, and relays the Ethereum co-founder's view of 22 October that cybersecurity's end state in the AI era may favour defenders but the transition will be hard, with formal verification and a minimal trusted computing base as the long-term answer. It states that not all the attacks it lists were launched by AI.

> 当然，并非上述所有攻击都由 AI 直接发起或执行，但 AI 正在同时放大攻击者与防守方的能力

_Curator's translation:_ "Of course, not all the attacks above were launched or carried out directly by AI, but AI is amplifying the capabilities of attackers and defenders at once."
