#### What the page says

_Curator's reading of the capture._

A US cybersecurity trade report (Cybersecurity Dive, 28 October) that the cyber-insurance industry is watching PHP2Shell for signs of a systemic event that could bring billions in claims beyond current risk models. It quotes AM Best researchers (report of Tuesday) that the PHP vulnerability is the systemic-risk scenario that has long worried insurers, and that concern has limited market supply while a high-profile exploitation event could also raise demand and extend liability to directors and officers; CyberCube calls PHP2Shell a 'watershed moment' for AI-driven flaw discovery and says the industry may have avoided a near-term catastrophe but the crisis could become a long-tail event, with slow-burning campaigns like this treated as attritional losses. It adds that researchers say actual impact has been very limited so far, that many agencies, companies and infrastructure providers disconnected sites, and that CISA added the flaws to its KEV catalog on Monday.

> The insurance industry is closely monitoring PHP2Shell amid concerns of a systemic event that could overwhelm the sector with billions in cybersecurity risk claims.
