#### What the page says

_Curator's reading of the capture._

A personal essay on note.com (Japanese) by the author 'yoshi', 22 October, arguing that AI shortened not the discovery of the PHP flaw but the time to demonstrate it was exploitable. Drawing on what it says are the Wiz Research, Calif and PHP primary pages, it says the weakness traces to PHP 5.4 (2012); that a related bug (PHP bug #78876, CVE-2019-11048) was reported in 2019 and handled as a disk-filling denial of service; and that a 2024 OSTIF/Quarkslab audit found a related out-of-memory issue but did not prove it exploitable, so it went unfixed. It quotes Wiz that AI cut the work from months by a world-class team to about one researcher-week, and says Calif and Wiz found the issue independently in August. It says it leaves out attack steps.

> AIが縮めたのは「発見」ではなく「悪用できると実証するまでの時間」だった。

_Curator's translation:_ "What AI shortened was not 'discovery' but the time until exploitability was demonstrated."
