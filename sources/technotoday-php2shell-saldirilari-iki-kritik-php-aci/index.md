_Curator's reading of the capture._

Technotoday (Turkey, Turkish), by Muhammed Kayan, dated 23 October on the page: how the two critical PHP flaws work. It says the CVEs were made more critical by technical details leaking before the 21 October update, that both score 9.2 on CVSS 4.0, that the first attack attempts were reported to have been seen before the official patches were released, and that the number of successfully compromised systems was not yet known.
