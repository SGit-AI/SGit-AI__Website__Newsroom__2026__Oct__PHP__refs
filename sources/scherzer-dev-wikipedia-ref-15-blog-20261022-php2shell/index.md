#### What the page says

_Curator's reading of the capture._

Daniel Scherzer's blog (English), 22 October: 'PHP2Shell: a Timeline'. The author is a PHP release manager (for 8.5) who says he was privy to internal discussions and lists only events and times verifiable from public information, mostly git history, GitHub logs and PHP Foundation posts, without judgement. Discovery: a 2024 security-audit tag in php-src (Quarkslab audit); a PHP developer in 2024 found the first signs of the out-of-memory problem; the PHP Foundation formed an Ecosystem Security Team on 18 May 2026; Calif began auditing PHP on 30 July under Patch the Planet, started finding code paths on 1 August, and on 11 August reported an unconditional remote code execution with the same cause as the 2024 finding, followed by reports from other organisations. Release: tags and tarballs for 8.2.35, 8.3.36, 8.4.27 and 8.5.12 were made on Monday 19 October (07:30 to 17:19 UTC) but not published; the release was due Thursday 22 October 14:00 UTC; about 15:00 UTC on Tuesday 20 October online discussion of the CVE IDs, with some wrong details, led to it being moved to Wednesday 21 October, same time; a pre-announcement was posted at 19:25 and 19:33 UTC; about 20:00 Calif published a proof-of-concept video, later deleted but copied (a copy was online at 20:27). On 21 October Chainguard published patched PHP before release (07:41 UTC per the PHP Foundation; Hacker News reports say builds were available from 06:07); the fixes were published between 13:31 and 13:35 UTC, with source-only backports for 7.4, 8.0 and 8.1. Fixes had been shared under embargo with several vendors beforehand.

> The coordinated releases were originally scheduled for 14:00 UTC on Thursday, October 22
