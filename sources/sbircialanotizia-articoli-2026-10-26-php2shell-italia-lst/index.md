#### What the page says

_Curator's reading of the capture._

An Italian magazine explainer (Sbircia la Notizia, 26 October) on ACN's alert, which per ACN as reported by Adnkronos covers more than 285,000 Italian sites; it stresses that exposure is a technical condition and that compromise needs separate evidence in each server's logs and files. It gives the patched PHP versions (8.5.12, 8.4.27, 8.3.36, 8.2.35), notes that the CMS update does not fix the PHP runtime, that the actual release serving HTTP must be checked (the command-line version can differ), that PHP processes must be restarted after updating, and that PHP 8.1 and earlier are out of support, with minimal source backports offered for 7.4, 8.0 and 8.1. It relays CERT-EU (install now, isolate exposed applications if that is not possible) and the UK NCSC (suspend public access for systems that cannot be updated or mitigated quickly), mentions an informational advisory of 23 October about a further, less likely flaw (CVE-2026-115560), and cites Reuters on an attempt Amazon saw against a decoy system on 17 October and other activity on 21 October before the embargo ended.
