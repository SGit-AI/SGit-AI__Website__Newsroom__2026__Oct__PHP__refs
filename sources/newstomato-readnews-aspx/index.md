A Korean finance-press piece (Newstomato, 26 October) saying Korea's Financial Security Institute is sharing information with the US FS-ISAC and Japan's F-ISAC, under a 2022 memorandum, about recent financial-sector hacking incidents and about the PHP vulnerability, with no channel to China. It reports that KB Kookmin Bank and Woori Bank were confirmed to use PHP and began remediation, and that the government raised the private-sector cyber crisis alert from 'attention' to 'caution' at noon on 22 October.
