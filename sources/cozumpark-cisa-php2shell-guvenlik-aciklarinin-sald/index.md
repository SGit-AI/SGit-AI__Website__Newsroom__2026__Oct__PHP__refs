#### What the page says

_Curator's reading of the capture._

A Turkish IT-news piece (ÇözümPark, 28 October) on CISA confirming that the two PHP2Shell flaws are exploited and adding them to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog on Monday (26 October), with federal civilian agencies told to secure their systems by Thursday (29 October) under Binding Operational Directive 26-04. It says the details leaked before the patches, that the PHP security team said none of the circulating proof-of-concept samples worked, that Cloudflare, Amazon, Hadrian, watchTowr, Previdian and Patchstack saw broad exploitation attempts before disclosure which they classed as failed, that Amazon said attackers began targeting the flaws four days before the patches, and that Perplexity's detection lead confirmed exploitation in real attacks over the weekend, on honeypots. It adds that CISA has not shared the scale of the attacks, gives VirusTotal's estimates (about 70% of sites on PHP, more than 5.1 million internet-facing PHP servers) and notes mitigations shared on 23 October for a further, not yet patched flaw, CVE-2026-115560.

> CISA, PHP2Shell açıklarını pazartesi günü KEV kataloğuna ekledi ve Federal Sivil Yürütme Organı kurumlarına sistemlerini perşembe gününe kadar güvence altına alma talimatı verdi.

_Curator's translation:_ "CISA added the PHP2Shell flaws to the KEV catalog on Monday and instructed Federal Civilian Executive Branch agencies to secure their systems by Thursday."
