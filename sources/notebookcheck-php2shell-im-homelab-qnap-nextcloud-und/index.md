#### What the page says

_Curator's reading of the capture._

Notebookcheck (German), 23 October, by Steffen Zahn: a guide to finding and patching the PHP2Shell flaws in home-lab software. QNAP published bulletin QSA-26-18 on 22 October with no fixed version yet and advises taking the NAS off the internet; Download Station and PHP Extensions will get no fix at all (support ended 30 September). OPNsense released 26.7.7 (Community, PHP 8.5.12) and 26.4.2p3 (Business, PHP 8.3.36) on Wednesday evening; Netgate shipped new PHP for pfSense, installable only by SSH or console. Synology had published no bulletin by 22 October. The article says watchTowr has seen active attempts since Wednesday but reported no successful break-ins, that the German BSI knew of no confirmed exploitation in its 21 October warning, and that QNAP says it knows of no attacks on its own devices. It also appears as a Polish edition (notebookcheck-php2shell-co-powinni-sprawdzic-uzytkowni).
