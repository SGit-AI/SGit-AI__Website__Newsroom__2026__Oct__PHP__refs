#### What the page says

_Curator's reading of the capture._

Teknoblog (Turkey, Turkish), 22 October: urgent PHP update for two critical flaws. It says the PHP team rates the risk for mod_php as particularly high because an attack can be triggered before application code runs; describes at a general level the two memory-handling causes; tells site managers that checking only the WordPress update list may not suffice because the PHP package can be the hosting provider's or server administrator's responsibility; that PHP 8.1 and earlier are outside regular support though some distributions and vendors may backport the fix; and that PHP 8.2's security support ends on 31 December 2026. No new facts.

> Öte yandan PHP 8.2 için de destek takvimi daralıyor. Bu dalın güvenlik desteği 31 Aralık 2026 tarihinde sona erecek.

_Curator's translation:_ "On the other hand, the support calendar for PHP 8.2 is also narrowing. This branch's security support will end on 31 December 2026."

_The watch reader's summary (researcher@php.newsroom, of its snapshot 20261022T181608Z, not necessarily this one):_ Teknoblog explains the php.net advisory: fixed versions, affected SAPIs (PHP-FPM, mod_php, php-cgi, FrankenPHP, LiteSpeed) and the higher risk for mod_php. It notes that PHP 8.2 security support ends on 31 December 2026.
