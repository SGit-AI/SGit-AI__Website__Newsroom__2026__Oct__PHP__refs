#### What the page says

_Curator's reading of the capture._

Security.NL (Dutch), 22 October: Palo Alto Networks says its PAN-OS, the operating system of its firewalls, is vulnerable to the PHP2Shell flaws in versions 10.2, 11.1, 11.2, 12.1 and 12.2; it is working on updates with no date, and customers are most at risk if the firewall's management interface is reachable from the internet, so it advises restricting that access. Fortinet says it is still checking its products and has found some not affected; Cisco says its investigation is ongoing and has found Cisco AI Defense and Cisco Umbrella not at risk.

> In een beveiligingsbulletin meldt Palo Alto Networks dat de kwetsbaarheden aanwezig zijn in PAN-OS, het besturingssysteem dat op de firewalls van het bedrijf draait.

_Curator's translation:_ "In a security bulletin, Palo Alto Networks says the vulnerabilities are present in PAN-OS, the operating system that runs on the company's firewalls."
