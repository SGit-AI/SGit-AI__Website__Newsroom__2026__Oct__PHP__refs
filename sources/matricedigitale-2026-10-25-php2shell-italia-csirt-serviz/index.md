#### What the page says

_Curator's reading of the capture._

Matrice Digitale (Italian), 25 October: CSIRT Italia's alert (AL01/1025/PHP2Shell) puts 285,149 PHP services on potentially vulnerable versions, 81.9% of the 348,130 services with an identified version. It recaps the two CVEs (9.2) and a third, CVE-2026-115560, for which no patch exists and the CSIRT recommends a vendor-published mitigation, and tells administrators, hosting providers and infrastructure managers to act at once. It notes the picture is mainly one of delay in updating internet-exposed installations. Same alert as the CyberSecItalia item (cybersecitalia-lalert-dellacn-in-italia-a-rischio-vulne), which gives the 741,523 total.
