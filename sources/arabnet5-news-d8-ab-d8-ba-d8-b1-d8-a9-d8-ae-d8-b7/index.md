#### What the page says

_Curator's reading of the capture._

An Egyptian general-news site (Arab Net 5) retells, in Arabic, a Wall Street Journal report on PHP2Shell. It says a Calif researcher found the flaw in August while reviewing PHP's source code with OpenAI's GPT-5.6 Sol under an OpenAI-backed programme for open-source projects, that Calif's chief executive contacted the PHP project's security engineer on 11 August, that Wiz's team independently found the flaw and its co-founder called it the most serious found with AI help up to then, that the PHP Foundation's security engineer called it among the most serious flaws the project had faced in over a decade and compared it to Heartbleed, that the team planned to release the fix on 22 October with details kept secret but a post on the Hacker News site revealed information before that date, forcing the announcement forward, and that Calif's chief executive warned people may use AI tools to compare security updates and turn what they find into ready-made attack tools. It stresses that a PHP flaw does not mean every PHP site is compromised or equally exposed.
