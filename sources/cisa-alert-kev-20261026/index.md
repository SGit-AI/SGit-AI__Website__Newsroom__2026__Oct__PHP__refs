#### What the page says

_Read in the curator's role._

CISA's alert of 26 October 2026 ("Release Date October 26, 2026"; the page gives no time; its `` element carries `2026-10-26T12:00:00Z`, the site's placeholder for a date, not a release time). It says CISA has added the two PHP flaws to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities (KEV) Catalog and updated its PHP alert of 21 October (`cisa-alert-php-20261021`):

> CISA has added two new vulnerabilities to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities (KEV) Catalog and updated the PHP Alert, Critical Vulnerabilities in PHP Require Immediate Patching:

> CVE-2026-109618 – PHP Memory Corruption Vulnerability

> CVE-2026-109619 – PHP Stale Internal Request-State Vulnerability

> These types of vulnerabilities are a frequent attack vector for malicious cyber actors and pose significant risks to the federal enterprise.

The rest is CISA's standard text on Binding Operational Directive (BOD) 26-04, which applies only to Federal Civilian Executive Branch (FCEB) agencies, and its encouragement to all organisations to prioritise KEV vulnerabilities.

What it does not say: it names no victim, no compromised organisation, no threat actor and no ransomware use, and it gives no due date. The due date (2026-10-29) and the ransomware field ("Unknown") are in the catalog itself, captured at the same pass as `cisa-kev-json` and `cisa-kev-catalog` (catalog version 2026.10.26, released 2026-10-26T22:24:24Z). A KEV listing means CISA holds evidence of exploitation; it does not by itself establish a named compromise.
