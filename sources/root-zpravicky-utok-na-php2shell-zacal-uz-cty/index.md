#### What the page says

_Curator's reading of the capture._

Root.cz (Czech), a short news item by Petr Krčmář, probably 22 October. It says Amazon recorded the first exploitation attempt on 17 October, four days before publication, on its MadPot honeypot network, on a server meant to attract attackers and not an ordinary production site, from an IP address linked to a commercial anonymisation network; Amazon cannot explain how the attacker learned of the flaws before disclosure. More attempts from various sources came several hours before publication, which may point to several actors; after the fixes attempts rose, but it is not certain every captured attempt could have succeeded on a truly vulnerable server. Amazon advises administrators to look for signs of attack from at least 17 October, not from disclosure day.

> Amazon doporučuje správcům při hledání známek útoku nezačínat až dnem zveřejnění zranitelností, ale prověřit podezřelou aktivitu nejméně od 17. října.

_Curator's translation:_ "Amazon recommends that administrators looking for signs of attack should not start from the day the vulnerabilities were published but check for suspicious activity from at least 17 October."
