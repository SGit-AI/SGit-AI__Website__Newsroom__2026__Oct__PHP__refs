#### What the page says

_Curator's reading of the capture._

A press release carried by CIO (28 October): the security-training company OffSec announced a free hands-on PHP2Shell lab in its Proving Grounds Play service to help practitioners examine and defend against the two flaws. The text recaps that Wiz researchers found the first flaw on 19 August and took over a Drupal site the next day and a WordPress site the day after, that PHP disclosed the flaws on 21 October, that Amazon calls PHP2Shell broadly exploited in the wild and researchers had confirmed attackers running commands on honeypots by 24 October, that CISA added both flaws to its KEV catalog on 26 October, and that on 23 October PHP disclosed a separate flaw, CVE-2026-115560, that can affect fully patched servers with an unusual session setup and had no fix yet. It also quotes two OffSec staff on attackers' use of AI.

> CISA added both flaws to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog on Oct. 26.
