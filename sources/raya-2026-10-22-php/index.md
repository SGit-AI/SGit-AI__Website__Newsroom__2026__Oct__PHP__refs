#### What the page says

_Curator's reading of the capture._

Al Raya (Qatar, Arabic), 22 October, a Q&A style explainer. It says two serious flaws were found in PHP, that authorities and IT-security experts treat the situation as an exceptional emergency because the details leaked unintentionally before the planned date, and that many site operators took sites offline as a precaution. Ordinary users face no direct risk to phones, tablets or computers, but an indirect one if a site they use is unpatched. It says it is not yet clear how the flaws were found though there are many signs that wide use of AI contributed, and that large hosting companies such as IONOS have already updated PHP on their systems.
