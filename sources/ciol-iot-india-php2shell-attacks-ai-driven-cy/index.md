#### What the page says

_Curator's reading of the capture._

CIOL (India, English), 25 October, by Deepali Jain: India recorded a 14% PHP2Shell impact rate against a global average of 10%. It cites Check Point Research ranking India 11th among 18 countries with the highest reported impact rates after disclosure, and repeats Check Point's regional and sector figures (Europe 14%; Asia-Pacific, North America and Latin America 6% each; telecommunications 20%, education 18%). Check Point compares the incident with Log4Shell, says details leaked before the planned disclosure and that screenshots of an attack tool have begun circulating. Datadog's regional vice-president says flaws unnoticed for years are now being found with AI, attackers have the same tools and vulnerabilities are often exploited within a day; the article closes with patching advice.
