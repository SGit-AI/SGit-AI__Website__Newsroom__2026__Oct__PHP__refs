#### What the page says

_Curator's reading of the capture._

ENTER.CO (Colombia, Spanish), 26 October, a piece headed 'the critical PHP flaws are already exploited on real servers'. It says that on Monday 26 October, five days after the patches, successful exploitation was confirmed: the case was documented by Adel Karimi of Perplexity with honeypots that recorded remote code execution against Apache with mod_php and PHP 8.4.26, in which the attacker obtained server memory and then ran a command; Marcus Hutchins of Expel called it the best-quality confirmation he had seen, and until then several security firms had seen only failed attempts or scans. It repeats the fixed versions (8.5.12, 8.4.27, 8.3.36, 8.2.35), the project's minimal source backports for 7.4, 8.0 and 8.1, mitigation measures for a third flaw (CVE-2026-115560) published on Friday that has no patch yet, and says Colombia's ColCERT issued critical alert AL-20261021-123 on 21 October, counting 8,699 internet-exposed PHP services in the country, mostly on ISP and telecom networks, a measure of exposure and not of vulnerable or attacked servers. It cites BleepingComputer, the PHP Foundation and Más Colombia as sources.

> El caso lo documentó Adel Karimi, de Perplexity, con honeypots (servidores trampa) que registraron una ejecución remota de código contra un Apache con mod_php y PHP 8.4.26.
