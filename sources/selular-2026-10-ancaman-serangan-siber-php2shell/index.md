#### What the page says

_Curator's reading of the capture._

Selular.ID (Indonesia), 24 October, by Suharno: Indonesia's Ministry of Industry (Kemenperin) temporarily switched off several digital services, including the TKDN (local-content) site, to head off cyber-attacks that exploit the two critical PHP2Shell flaws. The shutdown began Thursday 22 October at 22:00 WIB and was due to last until Sunday 25 October, announced on the ministry's social media as a risk-mitigation measure; affected services include the national industry information system (SIINas), the ministry's official site, the TKDN site and all kemenperin.go.id domains. On Saturday 24 October the TKDN site still did not load. The article stresses the TKDN site is a reference for the electronics and telecoms industry (product lists with local-content percentages).
