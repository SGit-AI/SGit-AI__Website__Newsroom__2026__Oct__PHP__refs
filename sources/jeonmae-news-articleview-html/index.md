#### What the page says

_Curator's reading of the capture._

A Korean regional-newspaper report (Jeonmae, 29 October) on Dream Research's report: attacks on firewalls in Korea, the United States, Japan and Taiwan on 23 and 24 October; the attacker scanned about 19,000 firewalls worldwide, chose 240 devices in the four countries, chained PHP2Shell on the firewall management servers with a Linux privilege-escalation flaw to gain top administrator rights on 24 firewalls, and left web shells on 22 of them; at a US government-related organisation two firewalls and then one internal management server were attacked, making 25 in all, with the extent of data leakage and further compromise unconfirmed and no Korean damage yet established. It adds signs hinting at Chinese links (target selection, a Chinese asset-search engine and a China-developed network tool, a time zone matching China Standard Time, Chinese-language prompts in an AI platform's logs) and that traces of an AI coding tool and settings for Chinese models were found, while Dream says there is no evidence AI wrote the attack code and the operation does not appear to have run autonomously end to end.

> 다만 AI가 공격 코드를 직접 작성했다는 증거는 확보되지 않았으며, 공격 전 과정이 AI를 통해 자율적으로 진행된 것으로 보이지는 않는다고 드림 리서치는 설명했다.
