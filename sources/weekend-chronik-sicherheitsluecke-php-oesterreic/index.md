#### What the page says

_Curator's reading of the capture._

An Austrian news piece (weekend.at, 30 October) that, more than a week after the PHP flaws became known, 28,622 Austrian websites still count as vulnerable according to a remeasurement by the Austrian cyber-risk firm Risikomonitor.com, with a reported patch rate of 18.1%; small businesses and sites with direct customer contact such as online shops and booking portals are most affected, and almost half of the attributable affected sites are in hospitality, retail, transport and catering. It adds that at disclosure about 3.9 million vulnerable sites with a visible PHP version were identified in the EU states plus Switzerland and Liechtenstein, of 34.9 million reachable sites examined, that a finding of a weakness does not mean a site was hacked, that Austrian sites were taken offline as a precaution on 21 October when the interior ministry knew of no successful attacks, and that the EU agency ENISA called for quick action, priority for public-facing systems and temporary isolation of exposed applications where an update is not possible.
