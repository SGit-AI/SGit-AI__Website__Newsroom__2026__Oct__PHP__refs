#### What the page says

_Curator's reading of the capture._

Polskie Radio 24 (Polish), 22 October, by Michał Tomaszkiewicz: BIP (public information) pages are going offline because of two PHP flaws. It says the Informacyjna Agencja Radiowa reported on 22 October that the Central Informatics Centre (COI) temporarily switched off BIP pages in the national public-information access system (SSDIP), affecting only some bodies, and that Białystok University of Technology restricted access to its systems; it stresses this is preventive, not an outage or a successful attack, and that restoring services may take days. It explains the two CVEs (9.2) and says the PHP team brought the release forward to 21 October after the premature disclosure on 20 October, and that Amazon's MadPot recorded the first attempt on 17 October.

> 22 października Informacyjna Agencja Radiowa podała, że Centralny Ośrodek Informatyki (COI) czasowo wyłączył strony BIP działające w Scentralizowanym Systemie Dostępu do Informacji Publicznej (SSDIP).

_Curator's translation:_ "On 22 October the Radio Information Agency reported that the Central Informatics Centre (COI) had temporarily switched off BIP pages operating in the Centralised Public Information Access System (SSDIP)."

_The watch reader's summary (researcher@php.newsroom, of its snapshot 20261022T181608Z, not necessarily this one):_ Polskie Radio 24 reports, citing IAR, that COI disabled BIP pages run in the centralised SSDIP system, and that Białystok University of Technology restricted its websites, USOSweb, Moodle and other services. It notes that the Czech Government Office and Czech meteorological services also went offline, and quotes Adam Haertle of Zaufana Trzecia Strona.
