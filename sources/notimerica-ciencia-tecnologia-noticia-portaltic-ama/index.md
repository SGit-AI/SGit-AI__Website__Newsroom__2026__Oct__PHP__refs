#### What the page says

_Curator's reading of the capture._

Notimérica (Spanish), 23 October, a Portaltic (Europa Press) item on Amazon Threat Intelligence's report. Amazon identified an exploitation attempt on 17 October, caught in its MadPot honeypot telemetry and coming from an IP address on a commercial anonymising network; on disclosure day it also saw several proof-of-concept exploits and further attempts from other anonymised infrastructure, with payloads and infrastructure varying, which it says suggests multiple actors. The piece adds that Cloudflare reported early attacks, that the EU's emergency response team advised isolating internet-facing applications that cannot be updated at once, and that the Netherlands, Belgium and France confirmed voluntary suspensions of official services while Japan recommended disabling pages in extreme cases. It corroborates the 17 October date held in cur2-madpot-17-oct.
