#### What the page says

_Curator's reading of the capture._

A Brazilian IT-management opinion column (TI Inside, 26 October, by the CEO of a Kubernetes-security company) arguing that PHP2Shell is fixed in the code but still exposed in production. It relays Wiz that PHP runs about 70% of websites and that AI models helped find the flaw and adapt the attack to WordPress, Drupal and Joomla within hours, says the CVE numbers leaked a day before the fix and the embargo was broken, cites Palo Alto's count of more than 22.6 million exposed instances, and describes last week's choice between staying exposed and going offline: Anatel took its SEI and other digital services offline, regional courts interrupted access, European governments did the same, and the NSA and CERT-EU advised switching off whatever could not be patched. The rest is advice on rebuilding container images, software inventories and supplier responsibility under Brazilian central-bank rules.

> Na semana passada, quem não conseguiu atualizar a tempo teve que escolher entre ficar exposto e sair do ar.

_Curator's translation:_ "Last week, whoever could not update in time had to choose between staying exposed and going offline."
