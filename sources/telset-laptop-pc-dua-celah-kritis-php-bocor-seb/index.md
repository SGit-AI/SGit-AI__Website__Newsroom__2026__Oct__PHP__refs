_Curator's reading of the capture._

Telset.id (Indonesian), 22 October: two critical PHP flaws leaked before the patch, via the Hacker News forum. It says PHP's advisory announced a patch at 14:00 UTC on Wednesday 21 October for two flaws rated 9.2 on CVSS 4.0, that the flaws are expected to affect PHP versions 5 to 8, that PHP reserved the right to release earlier if details of either became public or active exploitation appeared, and that no active exploitation had been reported at the time.
