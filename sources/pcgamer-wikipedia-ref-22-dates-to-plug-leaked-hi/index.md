_Curator's reading of the capture._

PC Gamer (English), 21 October, by Andy Edser, written the morning of the release. It says the PHP security team gave advance notice of two critical flaws each rated 9.2; that a video reportedly showing a tool called PHP2Shell executing code on web servers is circulating (via Ars Technica), so details seem to have leaked ahead of disclosure; that Sansec researchers say all web servers on PHP 5.4 or later are affected and expect mass attacks within 15 minutes of the release (due 14:00 UTC); that Cloudflare's CTO Dane Knecht said it was already updating firewall rules for early attacks, a stopgap only; and that watchTowr's CEO Benjamin Harris told BleepingComputer the two flaws had leaked from embargo and were 'CVSS10' and understood to affect PHP 5, 6, 7 and 8. Advice: inventory every PHP install, plan a maintenance window and back up.
