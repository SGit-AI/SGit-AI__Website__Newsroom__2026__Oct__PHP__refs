#### What the page says

_Curator's reading of the capture._

Tia Sáng (Vietnam, Vietnamese), a long feature, 'PHP vulnerability: AI turns what was forgotten into a threat'. It says many European bodies proactively disconnected or closed sites after information on the two flaws leaked before patches could be applied, quotes Professor Phan Duong Hieu of the Paris Polytechnic's telecom school on the unauthenticated remote control risk, says the flaws were found from August while the PHP team prepared patches quietly, that governments worry hackers could use AI to shorten exploitation, and that an estimated 1.5 million of 2.6 million servers still run unpatched PHP. The explanation of PHP and the AI discussion follow; no new facts beyond the Censys-derived estimate already held.

> Ước tính hiện có khoảng 1,5 triệu trong số 2,6 triệu máy chủ đang sử dụng phiên bản PHP chưa vá lỗi.
