#### What the page says

_Curator's reading of the capture._

A German financial-news piece (Börse Online, 22 October) on the PHP flaws and which shares benefit. It says Huntress, the PHP security team and Germany's BSI raised the alarm on Wednesday, that dozens of hospital and authority sites went offline as a precaution and the city of Mainz deactivated several services and its main portal, that the BSI rated the risk at warning level 3 / Orange (extraordinarily high) and said a temporary disconnection of the web server from the internet could be considered if patches cannot be installed quickly, which the article calls unusual, and that heise Security warned working exploits would appear within hours or at most days. It reports cybersecurity shares rising (Cloudflare nearly 7%, CrowdStrike 3%, Palo Alto 4%) while large e-commerce stocks held up.

> Alternativ kann eine vorübergehende Trennung des Webservers vom Internet bis zum Einspringen des Patches erwogen werden, um eine Kompromittierung zu verhindern, sollten Patches nicht kurzfristig installiert werden können.

_Curator's translation:_ "Alternatively, a temporary disconnection of the web server from the internet until the patch arrives can be considered, to prevent a compromise, if patches cannot be installed at short notice."
