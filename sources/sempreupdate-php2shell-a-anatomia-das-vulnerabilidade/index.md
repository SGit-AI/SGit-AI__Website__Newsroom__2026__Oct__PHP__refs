#### What the page says

_Curator's reading of the capture._

SempreUpdate (Brazilian Portuguese, a Linux news site), 23 October, by Emanuel Negromonte: PHP2Shell's anatomy and 'the failure of the Mirai botnet attacks'. It says proof-of-concept exploits became public within hours of the announcement and set off automated scanning, but that consolidated data from Sophos's Counter Threat Unit show the attacks documented so far, mostly aimed at installing a Mirai variant called Potassium, failed to compromise their targets, being stopped by network defences or by configuration dependencies; Sophos first classed some detections as successful compromises but revised that after forensic analysis. It adds that the PHP Foundation issued a supplementary advisory on 23 October confirming a third remote-code-execution flaw, CVE-2026-115560, with the fix left to the regular update cycle because the conditions for exploiting it are very specific. The exploitation conditions and scanner details it gives are left out of the notes.
