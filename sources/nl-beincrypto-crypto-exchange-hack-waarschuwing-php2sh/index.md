#### What the page says

_Curator's reading of the capture._

BeInCrypto Nederland (Dutch), 21 October, by Mohammad Shahid. It explains that the PHP2Shell patches (score 9.2) matter to crypto users, particularly small exchanges and payment services built on PHP, says Cloudflare has confirmed attackers already tried to exploit the flaws against its customers and that several European government sites went offline as a precaution, and quotes Wiz Research that a successful attack could expose passwords, authentication tokens and API keys. It lists categories it considers at risk (PHP payment gateways, smaller exchanges, DeFi and wallet front-ends) and says SlowMist's MistEye scanned PHP assets across the crypto industry. It states that no confirmed evidence shows the named services or major exchanges were hacked.

> Er is geen bevestigd bewijs dat de genoemde betaaldiensten of grote exchanges daadwerkelijk via PHP2Shell gehackt zijn.

_Curator's translation:_ "There is no confirmed evidence that the named payment services or major exchanges were actually hacked via PHP2Shell."
