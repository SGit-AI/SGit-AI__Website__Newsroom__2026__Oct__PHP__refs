TeknoTalk (Turkey, Turkish), 23 October: a piece built round a statement from the Turkish security firm Kron Teknoloji, which stresses that after patching, organisations should review who and what can reach critical systems (limited, logged privileged access and monitored critical sessions) to limit the effect of an attack.
