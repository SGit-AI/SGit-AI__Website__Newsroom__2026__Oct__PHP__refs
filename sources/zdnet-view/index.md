#### What the page says

_Curator's reading of the capture._

ZDNet Korea (Korean), 27 October, an interview with Theori CEO Park Se-jun in question-and-answer form on PHP2Shell. He advises operators to inventory PHP versions and update (8.5.12, 8.4.27, 8.3.36, 8.2.35), restart processes after patching, and check for compromise separately from patching; says ordinary users need not install anything and should change passwords only if told of a leak; says CVSS 9.2 does not mean the same as 10 because a working attack needs technical skill and the right environment, and that attempts are not breaches. He says Amazon saw attempts from 17 October, that Sophos and Palo Alto Networks first announced successful attacks and then corrected or withdrew them, that a Palo Alto-reported government breach was never confirmed to have started with the PHP flaw, and that as of 27 October no publicly verified breach of a real company or institution is confirmed, while unknown cases cannot be excluded. He cautions that public attack code often does not work and that AI lowers the cost of analysis for attackers and defenders alike, and is sceptical of a tool said to patch other people's servers.
