#### What the page says

_Curator's reading of the capture._

A Korean business-press analysis (Metro Seoul, 29 October) asking whether Korea is a target after the PHP flaws. It says attack attempts have continued worldwide, that industry reports no damage confirmed in Korea so far, that the Israeli research outfit Dream Research reported attacks aimed at 240 firewalls in Korea, the US, Japan and Taiwan and intrusion into administrator rights on about 25 US government servers, and that honeypots recorded attempts. It adds that, per the interior ministry's 2025 statistics, about 71% of 17,902 public-sector information systems use Java and only about 6% PHP, that small firms using PHP outside core systems are a worry, that the government raised the private-sector alert to 'caution' on 22 October and is considering emergency patch support for small firms, and that Korean security firms have released PHP2Shell check tools.
