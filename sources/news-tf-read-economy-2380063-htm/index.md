It relays Amazon's CISO CJ Moses's blog post that the 17 October attempt was caught in MadPot from an IP linked to a commercial anonymisation network, that Amazon saw several attackers on disclosure day, that it has not established how the attacker had the flaw information beforehand or whether any attack succeeded on a production server, and Cloudflare's figures (thousands in the ten hours before, tens of thousands in 45 minutes, hundreds of thousands in two hours, then millions).
