#### What the page says

_Curator's reading of the capture._

A Spanish tech-news explainer (Okdiario, 28 October) on how PHP2Shell was found with AI models. It gives the patched versions (8.5.12, 8.4.27, 8.3.36, 8.2.35), says almost every version since PHP 5.4 has at least one of the flaws and that the PHP project sees the greatest risk with Apache and mod_php, and sets out the discoverers' own timelines: according to Calif, OpenAI invited it into the Patch the Planet programme on 30 July, a Calif researcher found several attack paths on 1 August, Calif told PHP of the first flaw on 11 August, sent a demonstration three days later that PHP confirmed on 15 August, and filed a second report on 21 August; according to Wiz, it found the same flaws independently, the first on 19 August, and reported on 24 August. It reports Wiz's use of its own tool with two AI models, Wiz's comparison with Heartbleed and Log4Shell, and Calif's more modest account of the machines' role.

> El 11 de agosto, Calif avisó a PHP del primer fallo.
