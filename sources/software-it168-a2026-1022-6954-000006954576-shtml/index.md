IT168 (China, Chinese), 22 October: a vendor-written piece promoting '360 Security Cloud's' emergency protection plan for PHP2Shell. Its framing paragraph lists precautionary shutdowns on 21 October: in the Netherlands the Port of Rotterdam, the government portal Overheid.nl, the Dutch football association, the sites of the five national parties and several hospitals, following the national cyber-security centre's approach; in Finland the state IT agency Valtori took public government network services offline; France's interior ministry temporarily closed local-government administrative sites pending the patch; and Australian government sites, including the Signals Directorate's, were affected.
