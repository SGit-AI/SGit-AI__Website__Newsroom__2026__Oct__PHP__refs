#### What the page says

_Curator's reading of the capture._

Security.NL (Dutch), 22 October, updated: QNAP warns that its NAS software (QTS, QuTS hero, QuTScloud, Note Station, QNE, QVR Surveillance, TVR OS) contains the PHP2Shell flaws, rates the impact critical, is working on updates with no date and advises taking the devices off the internet (switch off port forwarding, UPnP, myQNAPcloud Link and the built-in web server; use a VPN if remote access is needed). An update says QNAP's own PSIRT has observed scanning and exploitation attempts using the flaws and is monitoring them closely. It corroborates qnap-products-affected and qnap-attempts-in-wild, already held.

> QNAP waarschuwt eigenaren van de NAS-systemen die het levert dat de PHP2Shell-lekken ook daarin aanwezig zijn en adviseert om de apparaten voorlopig offline te halen.

_Curator's translation:_ "QNAP warns owners of the NAS systems it supplies that the PHP2Shell flaws are present in them too and advises taking the devices offline for the time being."
