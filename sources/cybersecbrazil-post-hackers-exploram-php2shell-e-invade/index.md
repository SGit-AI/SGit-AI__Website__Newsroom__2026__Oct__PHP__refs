#### What the page says

_Curator's reading of the capture._

A Brazilian security-news roundup (CyberSecBrazil, 25 October) that gathers reports around PHP2Shell: Palo Alto Networks Unit 42's account of attackers compromising a European government entity (with signs of agentic AI use), CYFIRMA's report of a suspected North Korea-linked reconnaissance campaign monitoring 216 unpatched servers, Defused Cyber's honeypot probing, Cloudflare's note of millions of exploitation attempts, a lab reproduction by ReliaQuest, a third PHP remote-code-execution flaw flagged by the PHP Foundation (CVE-2026-115560), and a Bitsight count of about 37 million internet-facing PHP systems. The Unit 42 intrusion account is the one Unit 42 withdrew the same day (see cur20-scworld-unit42-retraction), so it is recorded as disputed.
