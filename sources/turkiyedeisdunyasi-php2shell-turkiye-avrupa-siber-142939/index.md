#### What the page says

_Curator's reading of the capture._

Türkiye'de İş Dünyası (Turkey, Turkish), updated 23 October: 'Turkey outdid Europe on the PHP2Shell threat', on an analysis by the fintech company Onlayer of about 1.4 million merchant sites in 20 markets. It says 35.8% of merchant sites in Turkey were affected by PHP2Shell against a 45.7% average abroad and 56.7% in Europe, that the share of updated sites rose about fivefold in under 24 hours after the fixes, that 36.2% of Turkish merchant sites use PHP (about 64% use other technologies and are unaffected) and that 98.9% of PHP-using sites were on affected versions, which it attributes to the flaw covering most PHP versions published since 2012.

> Araştırmaya göre Türkiye’deki üye işyeri sitelerinin yüzde 35,8’i PHP2Shell güvenlik açığından etkilenirken, yurt dışı ortalaması yüzde 45,7 olarak hesaplandı.

_Curator's translation:_ "According to the research, 35.8% of merchant sites in Turkey are affected by the PHP2Shell vulnerability, while the average abroad was calculated at 45.7%."
