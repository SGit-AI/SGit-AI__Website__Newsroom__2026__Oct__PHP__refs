#### What the page says

_Curator's reading of the capture._

En cause (French), 25 October, by Arnaud Virel. The capture holds the lead, the table of contents and the first section only (the rest sits behind a 'read more'). The lead says two critical PHP flaws allow remote code execution without authentication under some conditions, that the PHP project, CISA and CERT-EU ask for an immediate update to 8.5.12, 8.4.27, 8.3.36 or 8.2.35, and that CISA had not yet seen successful exploitation at its last update. The contents list a third vulnerability reported on 23 October and a section on no public proof of a successful attack at that hour; that text is not in the capture.
