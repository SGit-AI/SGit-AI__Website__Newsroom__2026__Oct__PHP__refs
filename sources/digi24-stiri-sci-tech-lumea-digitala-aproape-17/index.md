_Curator's reading of the capture._

A Romanian television-news report (Digi24) that is the same News.ro report as the Știrile PRO TV source already held: DNSC has notified 17,416 public-sector entities of the PHP2Shell vulnerability, the Government's General Secretariat says its platforms have the recommended measures applied, and the Romanian Police and Tulcea city hall sites showed precautionary maintenance notices.
