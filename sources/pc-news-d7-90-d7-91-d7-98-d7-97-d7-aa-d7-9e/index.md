#### What the page says

_Curator's reading of the capture._

An Israeli IT-trade piece (Anashim U'Machshevim, 28 October), headlined as a CISA emergency alert, reporting that CISA confirmed hackers are actively exploiting two severe PHP flaws, added them to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog and required US civilian federal bodies to apply the updates by 29 October. It relays Amazon's intelligence unit, via Tenable's blog, that first attack attempts were recorded on 17 October, four days before the emergency updates; Perplexity's detection lead's confirmation of a successful attack on a honeypot; a principal threat researcher at Expel calling it the best confirmation seen since the crisis began for servers on the Apache mod_php configuration; Cloudflare's technology chief's note that WAF rules for the known vectors were deployed to all customers as a stopgap before the patch release; and TechCrunch's account that Calif used AI models to work out how to exploit flaws once thought unexploitable.
